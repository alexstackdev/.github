# alexstack.dev

**A small product studio building useful digital products for everyday life.**

alexstack.dev is the independent technology organization behind a growing collection of apps, tools, and ideas. We design and build products with a focus on thoughtful user experiences, practical problem-solving, and continuous improvement.

## Our products

| Product | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **[alexstack.dev](https://alexstack.dev)** | Our home on the web and blog, where we share ideas, notes, and updates. |
| **MyHubt** | An app in the alexstack.dev product ecosystem. |
| **TooMuch** | A language-learning app designed to support the journey of learning languages. |

## What we believe

- **Build for real needs** — Start with problems worth solving.
- **Keep it useful** — Prefer clear, focused experiences over unnecessary complexity.
- **Learn by building** — Ship, listen, improve, and repeat.
- **Think long term** — Create products that can grow with their users.

## Open source

We believe in learning from the developer community and sharing useful work where we can. Repositories in this organization may include product code, experiments, shared tools, and supporting infrastructure.

Unless a repository includes its own license, its contents are not automatically open source. Please check each repository's license and contribution guidelines before using or contributing to its code.
